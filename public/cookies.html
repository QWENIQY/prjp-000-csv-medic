Cookie Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

This policy explains how CSV Medic, operated by {{LEGAL_NAME}}, uses cookies and similar technologies, as required by the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations 2003 (PECR).

The short version

We use one cookie. It is strictly necessary for the Service to work. We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies, and we don't let third parties set cookies on our website. Because our cookie is strictly necessary, the law doesn't require us to ask for your consent, which is why you don't see a cookie banner.

Cookies we use

NameSet byPurposeDuration
cm_did CSV Medic (first-party) Holds a random ID that links the files you prepare to your browser. This means only you can download them. It also applies the free daily download allowance. It contains no personal details. 400 days

The cookie is HttpOnly, which means scripts on the page can't read it. It is only sent over secure connections, and it is never shared with other websites.

Payments

When you choose to pay, you are taken to Stripe's secure checkout page at checkout.stripe.com. Stripe sets its own cookies there, which are needed for fraud prevention and to process your payment. These are covered by Stripe's cookie policy.

Managing cookies

You can delete or block cookies in your browser settings. If you block our cookie, the editor will still work. However, you may not be able to download fixed files, because we won't be able to confirm that a file belongs to you. Deleting the cookie does not reset the free download allowance.

Changes

If we ever add non-essential cookies (for example analytics), we will update this policy and ask for your consent before setting them.
