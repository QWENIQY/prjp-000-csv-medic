2. Summary

  • We don't require an account, and we don't use advertising or analytics trackers.
  • Your CSV is processed to fix it. We only store it if you choose to download, and then delete it within {{EXPORT_TTL_HOURS}} hours.
  • To limit free downloads, we store a one-way hash (a scrambled code) of your IP address and of a random browser ID. We delete these after 3 days.
  • Stripe handles payments. We never see your full card number.
  • We never sell your data.

3. Information we collect

a) Files you upload

Product CSV files normally contain product information (titles, prices, SKUs and so on), not personal data. If your file does contain personal data, for example a person's name in a vendor field, we process it only to provide the Service to you. Please don't upload customer data.

b) Technical information

  • IP address. We use it in the short term to protect the Service from abuse (rate limiting). We also store it, but only as a keyed one-way hash, to apply the free daily download limit. For IPv6 addresses, we hash the network prefix (the first half of the address) rather than the full address.
  • Browser ID. This is a random identifier stored in a cookie (cm_did). We use it to keep your files private to your browser and to apply the free limit. See our Cookie Policy.
  • Server logs. Like all websites, our hosting provider records basic request information (IP address, time, page requested, browser type) for security and troubleshooting.

c) Payment information

When you pay, Stripe collects your payment details, email address and billing country. From Stripe we receive and keep a payment reference, the amount, the currency, the date and the file the payment was for. We can also see your name, email address, card brand and the last four digits of your card in our Stripe account. We never receive your full card number.

d) When you contact us

If you email us, we keep your email address and the contents of the conversation so that we can help you.

5. How long we keep it

  • Files while you edit: processed in memory and not stored.
  • Fixed files prepared for download: deleted automatically after {{EXPORT_TTL_HOURS}} hours.
  • Hashed IP address and browser ID records (free limit): deleted after 3 days.
  • Rate-limiting data: held in memory for no more than 10 minutes.
  • Browser ID cookie: stays in your browser for up to 400 days unless you delete it.
  • Hosting server logs: kept according to our hosting provider's retention period, normally no longer than 30 days.
  • Payment records: 6 years from the end of the financial year they relate to, as UK tax law requires.
  • Emails with us: up to 2 years after your query is resolved.

6. Who we share it with

We only share data with:

  • Stripe (Stripe Payments UK Ltd and its group companies). Stripe processes payments and acts as an independent controller for fraud prevention and its own legal obligations. See Stripe's privacy policy.
  • Our hosting provider ({{HOSTING_PROVIDER}}), which runs our servers on our behalf as a processor under a written contract.
  • Professional advisers (such as accountants and lawyers), and authorities where the law requires it.
  • A buyer or successor, if our business is sold or reorganised. They would have to use your data in line with this policy.

We do not sell personal data, and we do not share it for advertising.

7. International transfers

Our servers are located in {{HOSTING_LOCATION}}. Stripe may process data outside the UK, including in the United States. Where data leaves the UK, it is protected by UK adequacy regulations (such as the UK Extension to the EU–US Data Privacy Framework) or by the UK International Data Transfer Agreement or Addendum.

8. How we protect it

All traffic is encrypted with HTTPS. Downloads use long random links that only work in the browser that created them. We store IP addresses only as keyed hashes, delete files automatically, and limit access to our systems. No system is perfectly secure. If a data breach is likely to put your rights at risk, we will tell you and the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) as the law requires.

9. Your rights

Under UK GDPR you have the right to:

  • access your personal data and get a copy;
  • have inaccurate data corrected;
  • have your data erased;
  • restrict how we use it;
  • object to our use of it based on legitimate interests;
  • data portability, for data you provided to us under a contract.

To use any of these rights, email {{CONTACT_EMAIL_LINK}}. We'll respond within one month. It's free in most cases. Please note that we store IP addresses and browser IDs only in hashed form, so we may not be able to link them to you unless you tell us your IP address and the approximate time you used the Service.

10. Automated decisions

Whether a free download is available is decided automatically, by checking whether the hashed IP address or browser ID has already been used that day. This doesn't have a legal or similarly significant effect on you: you can still buy the file, or try again the next day. If you think the check is wrong, contact us and a person will review it.

11. Children

CSV Medic is a tool for businesses and is not intended for anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect children's data.

12. Complaints

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your data, please contact us first. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office: ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint, telephone 0303 123 1113.
