Refund Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

We want every file you buy from CSV Medic to import cleanly. This policy explains what happens if it doesn't. It sits alongside our Terms of Service and doesn't affect your legal rights.

Our promise

If a file you paid for didn't work, contact us within 14 days of buying it. We will fix the problem or give you a full refund. This applies, for example, when:

  • Shopify rejects the fixed file because of something CSV Medic changed, or a problem that CSV Medic should have caught;
  • you paid but the download didn't unlock, or the file was empty or corrupted;
  • you were charged more than once for the same file.

What isn't covered

  • Problems we flagged as needing your decision (shown in red in the editor) that you didn't resolve before downloading.
  • Changing your mind after the file was unlocked. When you paid, you agreed to immediate supply, which ends the 14-day cancellation right for digital content. See our Terms, section 6.
  • Results of import settings you chose in Shopify, or edits you made to the file yourself.
  • Free downloads, because no payment was taken.

Your legal rights

If you're a consumer, you have rights under the Consumer Rights Act 2015. Digital content must be as described, fit for purpose and of satisfactory quality. If it's faulty, you're entitled to a repair or replacement. If that isn't possible, you're entitled to a price reduction, which may be a full refund. Nothing in this policy limits those rights.

How to ask for a refund

  1. Email {{CONTACT_EMAIL_LINK}} within 14 days of your purchase.
  2. Include the email address you used at checkout, or the receipt number from Stripe's email, and tell us briefly what went wrong. If Shopify showed you an error message, a copy or screenshot of it helps.
  3. We'll reply within 5 working days. Where we can, we'll fix your file first. If we can't, or you'd rather have your money back, we'll refund you.

Refunds go back to your original payment method within 14 days of us agreeing to them. Your bank may take a few more days to show the refund.
