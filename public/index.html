What it fixes

The errors that break Shopify imports — handled

CSV Medic repairs these automatically and flags anything that needs your judgement.

Handles & product grouping

Generates missing handles from titles, cleans invalid characters, fills handles on variant rows and warns when two different products share one.

Prices & numbers

Strips £, $ and € symbols, fixes "12,99"-style decimals and thousand separators, and clears compare-at prices that would never show a sale.

Variants & options

Sets "Title / Default Title" for single-variant products, moves option names to the first row and catches duplicate or missing variant values.

Image links

Converts Google Drive and Dropbox share links into direct image links, encodes spaces and flags local file paths Shopify can't download.

Encoding & garbled text

FAQ

Questions, answered

How do I fix my CSV file for Shopify?

Upload it to CSV Medic. It reads files in any common encoding and delimiter, renames columns to Shopify's exact headers, and fixes handles, prices, variant options, true/false values, inventory fields and image links. It then shows you a short list of anything that needs your decision. Review the changes in the editor and download a clean file to import under Products → Import in Shopify. To fix a file by hand, follow our 60-second checklist.

Which Shopify import errors does CSV Medic fix?

It fixes "Invalid CSV header: missing headers", "Illegal quoting on line", garbled characters, "Inventory policy is not included in the list", "Fulfillment service can't be blank", "Inventory quantity is not a number" and image URL errors automatically. It flags the problems only you can decide, such as duplicate variants ("options are not unique"), missing prices and handles shared by different products. See every Shopify CSV error explained.

What kind of CSV files does CSV Medic fix?

Shopify product CSVs, whether exported from Shopify, a supplier, Excel, Google Sheets or another platform such as WooCommerce. It accepts comma, semicolon and tab-separated files in UTF-8, UTF-16 or Windows/Excel encoding, up to 15 MB (Shopify's own import limit). It understands both Shopify's current column names and the older template.

Can I check my file before paying?

Yes. Uploading, fixing, reviewing and editing are always free. You only use your free daily download, or pay £5, when you download the finished file.

How does the free daily download work?

Everyone gets one free fixed-CSV download per day, reset at midnight UK time. After that, each extra file is a one-time £5 payment. There's no subscription and nothing to cancel.

Is my product data safe?

Your file is sent over an encrypted connection and can only be downloaded from the browser that uploaded it. It is permanently deleted after 24 hours. We never sell or share your data. Payments are handled by Stripe, so we never see your card details. See our privacy policy.

Will it change my product descriptions?

No. Descriptions are left as you wrote them. CSV Medic only repairs garbled characters caused by encoding problems and trims stray whitespace.

What if something can't be fixed automatically?

Some things need a human decision, like a barcode Excel turned into "1.23E+12", or two different products sharing a handle. CSV Medic highlights these in red with a plain-English explanation so you can fix them in the editor.

Can I get a refund?

If a file you paid for didn't work, contact us within 14 days and we'll fix it or refund you. See our refund policy for details.
